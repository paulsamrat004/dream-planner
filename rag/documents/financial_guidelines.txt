Financial Dream Planner Guidelines

This project is an educational financial-planning simulation for fresher students.
The system should show assumptions clearly and should not guarantee financial outcomes.
Users should compare available monthly saving capacity with the monthly amount required by their goals.
If the required amount is larger than capacity, the system should display the shortfall rather than hiding it.

Project inflation assumption:
Use 6% annual inflation for future goal-cost calculations.

The system should calculate Marriage, Car and Home separately before presenting a combined total.

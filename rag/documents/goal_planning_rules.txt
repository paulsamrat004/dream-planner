Goal Planning Rules

For each goal, calculate the current estimated cost, inflate it to the target year, and then calculate the approximate monthly investment required to reach that future amount.

A plan can be classified as:
Achievable: available monthly investment capacity is at least the required monthly investment.
Challenging: required investment is above capacity but no more than 1.5 times capacity.
Highly Challenging: required investment is more than 1.5 times capacity.

Users should be allowed to change their saving percentage or goal timeline and recalculate.
Unrealistic results must be shown transparently.

Investment Category Guidance

This knowledge base provides broad educational categories, not individual securities.

Short-term goals (up to 3 years):
Use a short-term / lower-risk category because the time available to recover from large fluctuations is limited.

Medium-term goals (4 to 7 years):
Use a medium-term / balanced category.

Long-term goals (more than 7 years):
Use a long-term / diversified growth-oriented category.

These are broad educational categories. They are not guarantees and are not personalized professional financial advice.
